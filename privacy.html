PeakCut Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 21, 2026

1. Introduction

PeakCut is a video clipping and content creation application. This Privacy Policy explains how information is handled when you use PeakCut and its connected services.

2. Information We Process

Depending on the features you use, PeakCut may process:

  • Video files and video links submitted for processing.
  • Audio transcripts generated from video content.
  • Application settings, creator profiles and generated clips.
  • AI provider API keys configured by the user.
  • Information required to connect social media accounts, including TikTok.

3. How Information Is Used

Information is used to process videos, generate short clips, create captions and titles, manage creator settings, and publish content when requested by the user.

4. Local Processing and AI Services

PeakCut processes video and speech transcription on the user's own computer or server. Audio transcription runs locally.

When an AI provider is configured, transcript text and related information needed for clip selection and title generation may be sent to that provider. The selected AI provider processes this information under its own privacy policy and terms.

5. TikTok and Other Connected Services

If users connect TikTok or another social media platform, PeakCut uses the authorization granted by the user to provide the requested integration features, such as publishing selected clips.

Connected platforms may receive account information and content necessary to perform these actions. Their own privacy policies and terms apply.

6. Storage and Security

PeakCut stores videos, generated clips, settings and application data in its configured storage location. In a local installation, this is normally on the user's own computer.

Users are responsible for protecting their devices, API keys and account credentials.

7. Data Retention and Deletion

Information remains stored according to the application's storage settings until it is deleted by the user or the storage location is removed. Users can manage their locally stored videos and clips.

8. Third-Party Services

PeakCut may interact with third-party services such as AI providers, video platforms and social media platforms. Those services process information according to their own policies.

9. Children's Privacy

PeakCut is not intended to knowingly collect personal information from children under the age required by applicable law.

10. Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated when application features or data practices change. The latest version will be published on this page.
